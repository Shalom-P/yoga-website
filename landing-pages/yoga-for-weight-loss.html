Good to know

Questions people ask first.

How much weight will I lose?

We don't promise numbers. Yoga supports an active, sustainable routine, and steady habits are what make the difference over time.

Is yoga enough on its own?

It's a great, sustainable part of the picture. Healthy eating and your doctor's guidance matter too.

I'm a beginner and not very fit — can I keep up?

Yes. Intensity is matched to you with modifications, and we build gradually so every level feels achievable.

Should I check with a doctor first?

Please do if you're inactive or have heart, blood-pressure, or joint concerns, and tell your teacher so we can plan around it.

How often should I practise?

Consistency beats intensity. Your teacher will suggest a sustainable rhythm you can keep.

+

Yoga supports your care — it doesn't replace it

These sessions support your overall wellbeing and are practised alongside professional medical care. They are not a substitute for diagnosis, treatment, or medication. Please tell your teacher about any conditions or symptoms so they can adapt your session safely, and keep your doctor in the loop.
