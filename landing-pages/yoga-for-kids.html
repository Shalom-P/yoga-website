Good to know

Questions people ask first.

What age is this for?

Sessions are adapted by age and mood. Share your child's age and we'll tailor the games and poses — typically school-age, but do ask us.

Does my child need to be flexible or sporty?

Not at all. It's playful and inclusive, and every child goes at their own pace.

Do I need to be present?

For younger children we recommend a parent nearby. Your teacher will guide you on what works best.

Is it safe?

Yes. We keep everything body-safe, avoid neck-loading inversions, and never push — fun always comes first.

How long is a session?

A 60-minute slot, though your teacher keeps younger children engaged with shorter, varied activities.

+

Yoga supports your care — it doesn't replace it

These sessions support your overall wellbeing and are practised alongside professional medical care. They are not a substitute for diagnosis, treatment, or medication. Please tell your teacher about any conditions or symptoms so they can adapt your session safely, and keep your doctor in the loop.
