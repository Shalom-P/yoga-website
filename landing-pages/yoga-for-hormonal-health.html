Good to know

Questions people ask first.

Can yoga balance my hormones or treat PCOS, thyroid, or menopause?

No. Yoga supports rest, calm, and an active routine alongside the care your doctor provides — it isn't a treatment for any condition, and you shouldn't change medication without your doctor.

Can I practise during my period?

Yes, gently. Tell your teacher and we'll adapt — often leaving out strong inversions if you prefer.

Is this suitable during menopause?

Yes. Many people find the calming, restorative focus and cooling breath soothing, and we adapt to how you feel each session.

Could I be pregnant — does that change things?

Tell your teacher and we'll switch to a pregnancy-safe approach, and ask for your doctor or midwife's clearance first.

How soon might I notice anything?

Everyone is different. Many people first notice calmer stress and better sleep, and consistency helps the most.

+

Yoga supports your care — it doesn't replace it

These sessions support your overall wellbeing and are practised alongside professional medical care. They are not a substitute for diagnosis, treatment, or medication. Please tell your teacher about any conditions or symptoms so they can adapt your session safely, and keep your doctor in the loop.
