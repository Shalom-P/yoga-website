The brand at a glance

Calm, personal, expert.

My Yoga Classes pairs every student with a certified teacher for live, one-on-one sessions. The brand should feel like a deep breath: warm, grounded, and quietly premium. Never loud or gimmicky.

Voice: warm and encouraging, plain-spoken, expert without being preachy. Speak to one person, not a crowd. Favor short sentences and verbs that feel calm: breathe, move, restore, show up. Avoid hype words, all-caps shouting, and crash-diet "transform your body" language.

DO

  • Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the circle "head".
  • Use teal-on-cream or cream-on-teal for maximum warmth and contrast.
  • Use the icon-only mark when the name appears elsewhere on the layout.

DON'T

  • Don't recolor the mark outside the brand palette or add gradients/shadows.
  • Don't stretch, rotate, or crowd the logo against busy photos.
  • Don't place the cream logo on light or low-contrast backgrounds.
Color

Palette

Deep forest green anchors the brand; warm coral is the single accent that drives the eye to actions. Cream keeps everything soft and editorial.

The 60-30-10 rule for ads: ~60% cream space, ~30% forest green, ~10% coral reserved for the one thing you want clicked. Coral should appear once per layout.
Typography

Type system

An elegant serif for headlines paired with a clean grotesque for everything else. Italics in coral add a gentle, human accent.

Headlines — Cormorant Garamond (Medium 500)
Find your calm, one breath at a time
Use for headlines, hero statements, big numbers. Set tight (line-height ~1.05), letter-spacing −0.012em. Italic + coral for emphasis words.
Body & UI — Hanken Grotesk (Regular / SemiBold)
Live, one-on-one sessions with certified teachers, booked around your schedule, from anywhere. Buttons, labels, captions, and body copy all use Hanken Grotesk.
Eyebrows & labels: SemiBold, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.18em. Body: Regular, line-height 1.55.
