# Marketplace listings: ready-to-paste copy

Drafted 2026-10-18 for the GEO audit (item O2). AI answers about online yoga in India cite superprof.co.in and teacheron.com most often, and UrbanPro often names teachers too. Being listed there is how the studio gets into those answers.

**Who signs up.** Superprof and TeacherOn are built around individual tutors: each teacher creates their own profile with their own phone and ID verification. UrbanPro also takes institutes, so the studio can list itself there. Claude can't create these accounts; the teachers or the owner have to.

**Rules for every listing:**
- Use the same facts everywhere: **My Yoga Classes**, **live 60-minute 1:1 online sessions**, **₹1,499 for one session, ₹6,999 for five, ₹9,999 for ten**, no subscription, sessions never expire.
- Every fact below comes from the website or the live teacher records. Don't add claims (results, cures, student counts) that the site doesn't make.
- No reviews written by us, and no paid or swapped reviews. Ask real students after a session.
- Each marketplace has its own rules about linking out and taking payment outside the platform. Read them before adding the website link or rates.
- Use a real photo of each teacher, the same one as on their profile page.

My Yoga Classes is a live, one-to-one online yoga studio. Every session is 60 minutes over video with a teacher who is watching only you.
