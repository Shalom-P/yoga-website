## 1. How the 185 were consolidated

**Merge rules applied.** Sanskrit and English variants of the same asana merged to one row targeting one URL, English phrasing kept in the H1 and Sanskrit in the H2 (`bhujangasana benefits` + `cobra pose benefits` + `cobra pose bhujangasana for back pain` → one row). British and American spellings merged to one page with both strings in body copy (`personalised` + `customized`; `one to one` + `1 on 1` + `1:1`). Clinical and consumer terms merged to the consumer phrasing (`hypertension` → `high blood pressure`; `geriatric` → `seniors` and `chair yoga`; `hormonal health` → `PCOS` and `menopause`). Condition head terms merged with their `online classes` modifier into a single row, because the modifier is what changes the SERP from publisher-owned to provider-owned.

**Conflicts resolved, with reasoning.**

- **`yoga for diabetes` / `yoga for high blood pressure`.** Researcher A rated both moderate difficulty and high opportunity on the existing condition pages. Researcher B rated both **hard and low**, do not target. B is right on the bare head terms and A is right on the modified ones. Verified: `myyogateacher.com/articles/yoga-for-diabetics` is **2,083 words** with `Article + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList + Person + ImageObject + WebPage` schema, sitting alongside Healthline; the hypertension SERP additionally holds Yoga Journal, Art of Living and `medicine.missouri.edu`. Against 858 to 907 words with no author, no citation and no FAQ markup, the bare terms are not reachable. The **`online classes for`** variants flip the SERP to `patanjaleeyoga.com`, `shyambhai.yoga` and `omyogainternational.com`, which are peer domains. **Resolution: target the modified commercial variants; demote the head terms to Tier 3.**
- **`yoga classes in my time zone`.** Researcher A called it a genuine differentiator worth a URL. Researcher B found the SERP is **entirely B2B scheduling software** (koalendar, lunacal, picktime, simplybook.me, youcanbook.me, bookeo, setmore). B names seven specific results with one coherent intent story, which beats A's intuition. **Resolution: excluded as a ranking target.** The narrower `yoga class time zone india to usa` survives at low priority as supporting copy, because its only competitors are two thin pages (`patanjaleeyoga.com/time-zones/`, `shyambhai.yoga/global-calendar-yoga-classes-online/`).
- **Pose pages: 107 vs 55 vs "hub-first".** Verified 107 entries / 55 unique / 35 groups / one-sentence descriptions. The hub-first researcher is correct. **Resolution: one hub, a small number of condition-scoped collection pages that can be assembled from existing text, and only the 5 most cross-referenced practices as standalone pages.**
- **`private yoga lesson cost`.** A said hard (thumbtack, lessons.com, gymdesk, offeringtree, brettlarkin). B said easy, because **"not one page is written for a buyer"**, they are all teacher-side "how much should I charge" pages. B's observation is the sharper read of the same SERP, and the corrected $9-11/session figure makes the answer genuinely newsworthy. **Resolution: Tier 2, hard-gated on USD pricing shipping first.**
- **PCOS: split or not.** Split. `patanjaleeyoga.com/online-yoga-classes-for-pcos-pcod/` and `yogiva.com/online-yoga-classes-for-pcos-pcod/` both rank on exact-match slugs from peer-strength domains, and patanjalee runs four separate PCOD time-slot products. The host page mentions PCOS only **x2** against menopause **x10**, so one page cannot serve both.

## 4. The three structural fixes that gate Tier 1 and Tier 2

Not keywords, but nothing above ships correctly without them.

**A. The condition-page title fix is code, not admin.** `app/(marketing)/classes/[slug]/page.tsx:32` currently reads `title: c?.name ?? "Class"`, pulling from `class_categories.name` in Supabase. Renaming the category in `/admin/classes` would retitle the page and simultaneously rewrite the nav label, the `/classes` hub card and every anchor. Instead add a `metaTitle` key to each of the 9 files in `lib/data/condition-pages/` (which are code, and are already the source of `metaDescription` on line 33) and change line 32 to `title: rich?.metaTitle ?? c?.name ?? "Class"`. One line plus nine keys, and it decouples the `<title>` from the UI label permanently. Remember `app/layout.tsx:66` appends ` · My Yoga Classes` (18 characters), so a `metaTitle` must be **42 characters or fewer** to stay under 60.

**B. FAQPage on the 9 condition pages.** Confirmed absent. The `faqs` array already exists in every condition JSON. Emit a `FAQPage` node next to the existing `courseJsonLd()` call at `app/(marketing)/classes/[slug]/page.tsx:48`, plus a `BreadcrumbList` (`Home → Class types → [condition]`), mirroring what `/teachers/[slug]` already does correctly. This makes roughly **45 existing Q&A pairs** eligible for rich results with no new copy, and it is the specific mechanism behind rows 3, 1, 4 and 17.

**C. `/pricing` needs a statically-rendered multi-currency table, not geo detection.** The page is prerendered (`x-nextjs-prerender: 1`, `x-nextjs-stale-time: 300`) and its cached HTML says `Prices shown in <!-- -->INR<!-- -->` with ₹999 / ₹4,499 / ₹7,999. Calling `cookies()` to vary this would kill ISR across the whole `(marketing)` group with no build error. Ship instead: (i) `plan_prices` rows for USD, GBP and EUR via `/admin/plans` so `effectiveCurrency()` stops downgrading; (ii) a static "prices in every currency we bill" table so the crawled HTML contains a dollar figure regardless of visitor geo; (iii) `Product` schema with three `Offer` nodes, `priceCurrency` per offer, `availability: InStock`, and a per-session `unitText`. Copy constraint: call them **prepaid 1:1 sessions**, never credits. Rows 7, 13, 15 and 24 all depend on this.

**D. Two smaller items the 185 candidates never mention.** `og:locale: "en"` at `app/layout.tsx:75` is not a valid locale and should be `en_US` with `alternateLocale` for `en_GB` and `en_IN`. And the homepage trust bar claims `4.9 · 1,200+ reviews` while `/reviews` renders 12, all from Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah. For a global push that is both a credibility risk and a geographic signal problem. Fix the claim to the honest number before adding any `AggregateRating` markup, because marking up 1,200 against 12 visible reviews is a structured-data violation, not an optimisation. Reviews are DB-driven (`reviews` table), so this is an `/admin/reviews` job, not a repo edit.

## 6. Keywords deliberately excluded and why

Fifteen queries that appear attractive in the raw list and should **not** be chased. Each exclusion names the disqualifying mechanism.

**Eaten by AI Overviews (settled consensus, near-zero click).**

- **`yoga vs pilates`.** Cleveland Clinic, Healthline, WebMD and Penn State hold the SERP with an identical answer. High volume, near-zero winnable click, and no commercial fit for an India-based 1:1 service. One researcher already marked it "explicitly deprioritize"; it should be removed outright.
- **`how often should you do yoga per week`.** Nine ranking pages converge on "2-3 times a week". Textbook AI Overview absorption. There is no differentiated answer available to a yoga studio.
- **`does yoga help lower blood sugar`.** SERP is `diabetes.org`, Healthline, UMass Memorial and three PMC papers. Pure YMYL consensus with maximum AI Overview exposure. Answer it inside the `/classes/diabetes` FAQPage once row 1 ships, and never as its own URL.
- **`alternate nostril breathing benefits`** (English form only). `health.clevelandclinic.org` at #1. Kept in Tier 3 for cluster purposes; excluded as a standalone target. The Sanskrit form survives.

**Wrong intent (the searcher is not a customer).**

- **`yoga classes in my time zone` / `yoga class in my timezone`.** Every result is B2B scheduling software: koalendar, lunacal.ai, picktime, simplybook.me, youcanbook.me, bookeo, setmore. The person typing this is buying booking software. This is the single most counter-intuitive exclusion in the set, because cross-timezone delivery genuinely *is* this business's hardest-won capability (`lib/timezone/index.ts`, device-detected IANA zones, the Asia/Calcutta alias fix). **Keep it as on-page reassurance copy on the market pages; never as a ranking target.**
- **`online yoga therapy sessions`** (bare). Commercial intent is corrupted by certification demand: premayogainstitute, breathingdeeply, theyogatherapyinstitute, soulofyoga, pryt.com and school.myvinyasapractice all sell **training**, not sessions. Attaching a condition (`yoga therapy for diabetes online`, row 16) strips the noise and restores buyer intent. The bare term does not.
- **`myyogateacher pricing`.** Brand-navigational. The competitor correctly holds position one on its own brand. Third-party pricing pages do rank, so there is nominal room, but the traffic is people checking a bill, not people choosing a provider. It converts poorly. The `alternative` framing (row 24) captures the same demand at the point where the searcher is actually shopping.
- **`find a yoga teacher online`.** Brand-adjacent to `myyogateacher.com`, which owns the phrase as a matter of exact-match brand identity. Superprof and directories fill the rest. Treat `/teachers` as an entity page, not a ranking target for this string.
- **`private yoga lesson cost` teacher-side variants** (`how much to charge for yoga classes`, `yoga instructor rates`). Same words, opposite audience. Every ranking page is written for teachers setting prices. Row 15 targets the buyer-side phrasing only; the teacher-side phrasings would attract traffic that will never book.

**Unwinnable by structure.**

- **`online kids yoga classes`.** `outschool.com/online-classes/yoga` at position one with a 140,000-class marketplace, plus cult.fit. Marketplace territory. Invest nothing beyond the Tier 1 retitle. The **`1 on 1 online kids yoga`** qualifier removes Outschool and cult.fit from the SERP entirely and leaves only `shyambhai.yoga/product/kids-yoga-online-classes/`, which is the pattern to exploit; the unqualified head is not.
- **`downward dog adho mukha svanasana modifications`.** Saturated head term. Build only if the `/poses` hub needs it for internal-link completeness and to support a wrist-pain page. Expect no ranking.
- **`types of yoga explained`.** myyogateacher runs 46 `/types-of-yoga` URLs. Encyclopaedic, low commercial intent. Worth existing as an internal routing surface if and only if the pose hub ships first; worth zero promotion either way.

**Off-business, or a claim this site cannot make today.**

- **`online yoga classes for anxiety` / `yoga for anxiety`.** Two independent disqualifiers. The SERP is dominated by yoga-therapy certification courses rather than consumer services, **and** migration `0024_category_copy_positive` deliberately stripped clinical hedging and therapeutic claims from these pages under UAE and India advertising rules. Verified on the live page: `/classes/mental-health` contains `"anxiety"` **x0** and `"stress"` x11. The copy was built to avoid this term on purpose. **Target stress relief (row 11) and never anxiety.** Reintroducing the word would undo a deliberate compliance decision.
- **`book a yoga teacher online in my currency`.** No competitor addresses currency in ranking copy, so it looks like a clean differentiator. It is not one the site can honestly claim: the prerendered `/pricing` HTML contains only ₹999, ₹4,499 and ₹7,999 plus the literal string `Prices shown in INR`, and `GET /api/region` returns INR. The promise would be false until USD, GBP and EUR `plan_prices` rows exist. **Revisit only after §4C ships.**
- **`cheap 1 on 1 online yoga`.** Excluded now, viable later, and worth flagging because the corrected pricing changes the verdict. 1om1.net ranks on "from Only $10" and mywowfit on "$24.50 per session". At a true **₹899.80 (~$10) per session on the 5-pack** this site is competitive with the cheapest result on the page, not the most expensive. But the query is unaddressable while the indexed HTML shows rupees. Move it into Tier 2 the same week USD renders.
- **`yoga tailored to my body`.** Conversational long tail with negligible volume. The site's own H1 pattern "shaped around you" is close and already deployed across all nine condition pages. Use as supporting copy on `/private-online-yoga-classes`; do not give it a URL.
- **Any free-trial or "no credit card" keyword.** Not in the raw list, and deliberately not added. Public copy has had this wording stripped on purpose (PR #49), while the backend still grants a free first session via `isFreeTrial` in `app/api/bookings/confirm/route.ts`. The copy and backend disagree by design. An SEO recommendation is not the place to re-open that decision, and any page built from this table must keep the "1:1" framing without a trial offer.
