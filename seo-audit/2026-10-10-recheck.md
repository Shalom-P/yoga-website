# SEO Audit Recheck: myyogaclasses.fit

**Run:** 2026-10-10 (three weeks after the 2026-09-16 audit in `SEO-AUDIT.md`)
**Method:** every public URL fetched live as Googlebot, Google and Bing checked in a real browser, Lighthouse 12 mobile runs on `/` and `/classes/diabetes`, keyword SERPs re-sampled by web search.
**Data caveat:** no Ahrefs, Semrush or Search Console connector is authorized. Volume and difficulty are SERP-derived estimates, not tool data. Authorize the Ahrefs MCP in claude.ai connector settings to upgrade the keyword table.

---

## Executive summary

The site has moved from "not in any index" to "barely in Google". `site:myyogaclasses.fit` now returns two URLs (the homepage and `/legal/terms`, the latter last crawled 21 Jun 2026). Bing could not be checked directly (CAPTCHA), but DuckDuckGo, which is Bing-backed, lists eight of the 23 URLs, so Bing has very likely indexed the site. The September technical and on-page roadmap is largely done: the condition pages carry the planned titles, every page has an Organization `@id`, FAQPage and BreadcrumbList are on the right pages, teacher pages emit `Person` with `hasCredential`, fabricated reviews and ratings are gone, the GitHub repo points at the live site, and Lighthouse SEO scores 100 on both sampled pages.

Biggest strength: technical hygiene is now better than every competitor sampled (clean 23-URL sitemap, CSP, HSTS, structured data that validates, no fabricated social proof).

Top three priorities:
1. **Get the other 21 URLs indexed.** Google has the homepage but only one deep page, and that one is the terms page. This is a Search Console job (sitemap submission plus URL Inspection on the nine condition pages and three teacher pages), not a code job.
2. **Earn the first inbound links.** Two weeks of fresh searching found zero third-party pages that link to or mention the domain. Nothing outside the GitHub repo references it. Without links, a four-month-old domain stays in "Discovered, currently not indexed" for deep pages.
3. **Fix the homepage LCP.** The H1 is the LCP element and it paints at 5.8 s on simulated mobile because the hero fades in after 1.5 MB of JavaScript hydrates. Everything else on performance is fine (TBT 20 ms, CLS 0).

Overall assessment: **strong technical foundation, no authority yet.** The constraint is discovery and trust, exactly as the September audit said. The difference is that the on-site excuses are gone.
